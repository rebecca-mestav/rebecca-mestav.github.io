Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Alphabet Snake app ("the game") uses and why. The game is made by Bec, an independent developer. If you have any questions, email beckurogames@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • Your scores, favourite words and settings are kept on your own device, with a backup copy online once you have an account.
  • If you sign in (with Google Play Games, your Google account or, on iPhone, your Apple ID), the username you choose, your scores and the words you find are sent to the game's online lists so other players can see them.
  • You can delete your account and online information at any time, in the game or by email.

Information kept on your phone

The game saves your high scores, the words you have found, your favourite words, the cosmetics you have unlocked and your settings (such as language, music and sound volume) on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this copy.

Google Play Games sign-in

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google, and your best scores are posted to the game's Play Games leaderboards. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy.

Sign in with Google

You can also sign in with your Google account, in the browser or on your phone (signing in is needed to choose a username). If you do, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account's email address, name and a Google account ID from Google. These are used only to sign you in and keep your account the same on every device; they are never shown to other players.

Sign in with Apple

On iPhone, you can sign in with your Apple ID instead (signing in is needed to choose a username). If you do, Firebase Authentication receives an Apple account ID and an email address from Apple, which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email. These are used only to sign you in; they are never shown to other players. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Online backup

Once you have a username, a copy of your high scores, words found, favourite words and unlocked cosmetics is saved in Firebase, linked to your account, so you don't lose them on a new phone or browser. On Android, a copy is also saved in your Google Play Games saved games. Only you can read your backup.

Online high scores and word lists

When you are signed in, the game uses Google Firebase (a service run by Google) to keep global lists. To do this it stores:

  • the username you choose, linked to your Play Games player ID, Google account or Apple ID;
  • your game scores and the words in each game, with the date;
  • which words you were the first to find, and how many times you have eaten each word;
  • your Versus match totals (wins, losses and draws);
  • a random ID that Firebase creates for your copy of the game, used to keep other people from changing your records.

Your username, scores, words and Versus win rate are shown publicly to other players of the game. Please don't use your real name or personal details as your username.

Playing in a web browser

The browser version of the game keeps your scores, words and settings in your browser's own storage for this website, on your device only. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes them. Google Play Games is not used in the browser; you can sign in with your Google account instead, and the same online lists, backup and Versus matches described here apply.

Online Versus matches

When you play a match online, the game uses Firebase Realtime Database to share the match with the other player: a match code, the random Firebase ID of each player, and the moves and letters in the match. This information is only used to run the match.

Permissions

The game uses internet access for online features and vibration for game effects. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Who processes your information

Online information is stored by Google (Firebase and Google Play Games Services) on the game's behalf and is sent over encrypted connections. You can read how Google handles it in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. It is not shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Online scores and word records are kept while the game's global lists are running. To delete your account and data, open the game, tap the account button in the top right corner of the menu, then Delete account. This deletes your username, your games and match ratings, your likes, your mail and your online backup straight away, takes your username off the words you were the first to find or have eaten the most (the words themselves stay on the list), and deletes your sign-in. If you signed in with Apple, the game's access to your Apple ID is revoked too. You can also email beckurogames@gmail.com with your username and the subject "Delete my data". Your account, username, online backup, scores and word records will be deleted within 30 days. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires it. You can delete what's saved on your device yourself with Settings > Delete user data in the game. You can also stop sending information at any time by signing out or uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created a username, email us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
